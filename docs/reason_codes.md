# TraceMesh Canonical Reason-Code Dictionary (AML Compliance)

This document provides the standardized dictionary of behavioral anomaly codes, SHAP feature attributions, and regulatory compliance narratives used across the TraceMesh Mule Account Detection platform.

Every alert and risk score generated by TraceMesh is accompanied by deterministic reason codes mapped directly to institutional Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Suspicious Activity Report (SAR / STR) standards.

---

## 1. Reason Codes Overview

Each reason code includes:
- **Code**: Unique programmatic identifier.
- **Category**: Structural Graph, Flow Velocity, Account Profile, or Counterparty Contagion.
- **Direction**: `increases_risk` (+) or `decreases_risk` (-).
- **Default Weight**: Normalized contribution towards fused risk score.
- **Compliance Narrative Template**: Standardized regulatory description with variable placeholders.
- **Analyst Action Guide**: Prescribed operational triage step for investigators.

---

## 2. Structural & Typology Reason Codes

### `CYCLE_DETECTED` (or `circular_flow`)
* **Category**: Structural Graph Topology
* **Direction**: `increases_risk`
* **Default Weight**: `+35.0`
* **Compliance Narrative**:  
  > "Circular fund routing: ₹{amount} transferred across {hops} intermediary accounts before returning to origin account within {duration_hours} hours ({laps} completed laps)."
* **Trigger Condition**: Directed cycle of length 3–5 within a 24-hour rolling window.
* **Analyst Action Guide**: Place immediate debit freeze on intermediary conduit accounts; identify terminal cash extraction points.

### `BURST_INFLOW_RATIO` (or `fan_in_burst`)
* **Category**: Flow Velocity & Aggregation
* **Direction**: `increases_risk`
* **Default Weight**: `+30.0`
* **Compliance Narrative**:  
  > "Inflow velocity anomaly: Received ₹{amount} from {count} distinct senders within {window_min} minutes ({multiplier}x normal baseline volume)."
* **Trigger Condition**: Account aggregates transfers from $\ge 3$ distinct accounts within a 60-minute window exceeding ₹20,000 total volume.
* **Analyst Action Guide**: Scrutinize incoming counterparty origins; determine if incoming accounts belong to cyber-fraud victims or smurfing nodes.

### `RAPID_PASS_THROUGH` (or `rapid_pass_through`)
* **Category**: Flow Velocity & Transit
* **Direction**: `increases_risk`
* **Default Weight**: `+30.0`
* **Compliance Narrative**:  
  > "High-velocity pass-through: {forward_pct}% of inbound funds (₹{amount}) forwarded to destination accounts within {median_delay_min} minutes of receipt."
* **Trigger Condition**: Forwarding ratio $\ge 85\%$ of total inbound funds within a 15-minute median delay.
* **Analyst Action Guide**: Check retention balance. Accounts retaining $< 5\%$ of high inbound volumes indicate classic mule forwarding transit.

### `FAN_OUT_DISPERSAL` (or `fan_out_split`)
* **Category**: Structural Flow & Dispersion
* **Direction**: `increases_risk`
* **Default Weight**: `+25.0`
* **Compliance Narrative**:  
  > "Fund smurfing/dispersal: Disbursed ₹{amount} into {count} smaller outbound payments to distinct counterparties within {window_min} minutes."
* **Trigger Condition**: Single source disperses funds to $\ge 3$ distinct recipient accounts or ATMs within 30 minutes.
* **Analyst Action Guide**: Trace outbound beneficiary accounts for rapid ATM cash withdrawals or off-ramp conversion into digital wallets.

### `LAYERING_CHAIN` (or `layering_chain`)
* **Category**: Structural Graph Topology
* **Direction**: `increases_risk`
* **Default Weight**: `+30.0`
* **Compliance Narrative**:  
  > "Multi-hop layering sequence: Funds moved across {hops} sequential accounts with {decay_pct}% value decay between hops, obfuscating original fund origin."
* **Trigger Condition**: Sequential linear transfers of similar amounts ($\pm 15\%$) across 3 or more consecutive intermediary accounts.
* **Analyst Action Guide**: Trace the head node (victim/origin) and tail node (collector/sink); issue stop-payment notices along the active chain.

### `DORMANT_ACTIVATION` (or `dormant_active`)
* **Category**: Account Profile & Velocity
* **Direction**: `increases_risk`
* **Default Weight**: `+25.0`
* **Compliance Narrative**:  
  > "Sudden dormancy exit: Account inactive for {dormant_days} days moved ₹{amount} across {txn_count} transactions within {active_hours} hours."
* **Trigger Condition**: Zero outbound activity for $\ge 90$ days followed by sudden transaction volume $\ge 5\times$ historical baseline.
* **Analyst Action Guide**: Verify whether account credentials were compromised, sold, or rented to mule syndicates.

## 3. Network & Profile Reason Codes

### `NEIGHBOR_CONTAGION`
* **Category**: Counterparty Contagion
* **Direction**: `increases_risk`
* **Default Weight**: `+20.0`
* **Compliance Narrative**:  
  > "Network proximity risk: Directly transacted with {mule_neighbors} high-risk flagged accounts with an aggregate exposure of ₹{exposure_amount} (contagion score {contagion_pct}%)."
* **Trigger Condition**: 1-hop counterparty network risk average $\ge 40\%$.
* **Analyst Action Guide**: Review historical transaction counterparties for common IP, device fingerprints, or shared beneficiary IFSC codes.

### `SINK_EXTRACTION` / `ATM_EXTRACTION`
* **Category**: Fund Exfiltration
* **Direction**: `increases_risk`
* **Default Weight**: `+25.0`
* **Compliance Narrative**:  
  > "Terminal cash exfiltration: ₹{amount} ({cashed_pct}% of ring volume) siphoned through cash-out ATM terminals or untraceable sinks."
* **Trigger Condition**: Outflow directed to designated cash withdrawal sinks or untracked off-ramps.
* **Analyst Action Guide**: Contact ATM operations/surveillance for CCTV footage retrieval at cash-out terminal timestamps.

### `MINIMAL_KYC_HIGH_FLOW`
* **Category**: Account Profile
* **Direction**: `increases_risk`
* **Default Weight**: `+15.0`
* **Compliance Narrative**:  
  > "KYC profile mismatch: Minimal KYC tier account handling ₹{amount} volume, substantially exceeding declared income bracket ({declared_income})."
* **Trigger Condition**: Minimal KYC status paired with transaction volume $> 3\times$ upper bound of declared income band.
* **Analyst Action Guide**: Initiate mandatory KYC re-verification; restrict daily outward remittance limits.

## 4. Mitigating & Benign Reason Codes (Negative Weights)

### `NORMAL_PROFILE`
* **Category**: Mitigating Profile
* **Direction**: `decreases_risk`
* **Default Weight**: `-20.0`
* **Compliance Narrative**:  
  > "Established customer history: Active account for {account_age_years} years with consistent biometric verification and stable counterparty relationships."
* **Analyst Action Guide**: Low priority; no immediate freezing required.

### `RETAIL_MERCHANT_PROFILE`
* **Category**: Benign Commercial Behavior
* **Direction**: `decreases_risk`
* **Default Weight**: `-35.0`
* **Compliance Narrative**:  
  > "Legitimate commercial retailer: High inflow volume (₹{amount}) distributed across {count} customers with substantial fund retention ({retention_pct}%), matching verified merchant category."
* **Analyst Action Guide**: Confirm merchant registration; dismiss false-positive alert if retention and ticket distribution match grocery/retail benchmarks.

### `TERMINAL_SINK`
* **Category**: System Terminal Node
* **Direction**: `decreases_risk`
* **Default Weight**: `-50.0`
* **Compliance Narrative**:  
  > "Designated terminal endpoint: Legitimate cash-dispensing ATM or interbank settlement gateway."
* **Analyst Action Guide**: System endpoint; do not flag as an individual suspect mule account.

## 5. Standard Recommended Actions by Risk Score Tier

| Score Range | Severity Tier | Recommended Analyst Action |
|---|---|---|
| **$\ge 80.0$** | **Critical** | Immediate freeze of all collector and mule accounts; initiate cyber-cell STR/SAR regulatory filing; issue inter-bank recall notices. |
| **$50.0 - 79.9$** | **Medium / High** | Place temporary debit freeze on intermediary conduit accounts; escalate case to senior AML investigator; request transaction proof-of-purpose. |
| **$< 50.0$** | **Low / Review** | Enhanced transaction monitoring for 48 hours; place automated hold on single-day transfers exceeding threshold; request KYC re-verification. |
